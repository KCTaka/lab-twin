# CAD pilot: 8 Thorlabs parts (2026-10-09)

Eight STEP files were downloaded in a browser at human pace (about 30 s apart; Thorlabs'
robots.txt disallows automated STEP fetching), ingested with `s2_ingest_downloads.py`,
converted headless (HOOPS, 2-4 s each), read independently with OpenCASCADE, authored,
and validated in PhysX. The worklist is in `docs/worklists/cad_pilot_thorlabs.md`.

Masses use 6061 aluminium (2700 kg/m^3) unless the description states the material
(TR1: "SS", so 7930). Mixed-material assemblies (KM100, PT1, MAX311D contain steel
screws, balls, micrometers and piezos) are therefore underestimates and stay flagged
`physics.density_kg_m3: assumed`. The upper bound from the product page is consistent:
KM100 computed 55.7 g against a 0.24 lb (109 g) package weight.

## What real vendor files broke (and what changed)

1. **Orientation varies per file.** All 8 come from SolidWorks, yet 7 are Y-up and
   MAX311D is Z-up, so neither the header nor a fixed convention is enough. The
   geometric score (stable flat base area, a centred post tap, the native up-axis prior)
   proposed 6 of 8 correctly. PT1 was proposed upside down, because its top platform is
   larger than its base. KM100 is unresolved: its as-used pose (on a post) is not
   statically stable, and its post tap is on a 45 deg axis. Orientation is now a
   **reviewed decision**: `scenes/orientation_sheet.py` renders all six poses, and the
   reviewer records `cad.down_axis` plus `cad.orientation_review` in the spec. I
   (Claude) reviewed these 8 from the renders; a human should confirm them, and KM100
   needs a look at a real unit.
2. **File units differ.** 4 of 8 (PH3, CP02, SM1L10, KM100) convert in inches
   (`metersPerUnit = 0.0254`), the others in mm. S4 handles any `metersPerUnit`, and the
   frame check compares both readers in mm.
3. **A surface-only export.** CP02 has 0 closed solids, so its mass comes from mesh
   volume (flagged `mass_basis: mesh`).
4. **Large assemblies.** MAX311D has 301 meshes and 482k triangles. The collision budget
   gives meshes under 0.5 % of the volume (screws, balls, pins) no collider, and convex
   decomposition only to concave meshes of at least 5 %. Result: 26 colliders
   (4 decompositions) instead of 185 decompositions.
5. **Key-point rules.** "Largest face at the very top" picked knob and micrometer tips
   (3-36 mm^2). The support face is now the highest upward face of at least 10 % of the
   footprint, and probes are sized to fit it. A probe that rests *above* a face (taller
   geometry inside its footprint) is reported as obstructed rather than failed; falling
   *below* still fails.
6. **Thread guesses are diameter-only.** 1/4"-80 adjuster bushings match "1/4-20" (same
   6.35 mm major diameter). Thread pitch cannot be recovered from a cylinder radius,
   so guesses are hints, not identifications.
7. **Bugs the independent checks caught.** A shadowed variable recorded the rotation
   axis as `[1]` (C2 failed on 5 parts, and there is now a Y-up fixture regression test),
   and S4 did not re-author when only the CAD analysis changed (staleness now hashes it).

Negative controls (collision disabled): every probe fails, for all 8 parts and the 3 fixtures.
